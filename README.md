## ✨ Key Technical & Art Highlights

* 🏞️ **Open-World Level Design:** Custom landscape sculpting, biome placement, atmospheric fog, dynamic lighting, and reflection tuning.
* 💻 **C++ & Blueprints Logic:** Playable character setup, collision bounds, interactive environment triggers, and custom camera behavior.
* 💥 **Niagara VFX & Shaders:** Custom water physics, glare/lens flare adjustments, particle systems, and environmental effects.
* 📦 **3D Pipeline & Asset Optimization:** Asset modification and custom sculpting via Blender & Maya. LOD (Level of Detail) management and texture budget optimization for smooth FPS performance.
* 🕹️ **Fully Playable Build:** Optimized lighting and collision meshes allow real-time character navigation across the entire map.
